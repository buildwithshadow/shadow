# Repository Instructions

## Private Team Planning

Keep internal team roadmaps, accelerator or grant planning, milestone calendars,
submission drafts, and recording notes outside this public repository. Do not
commit, publish, or link to those artifacts unless the user explicitly authorizes
that exact artifact for publication. This applies to archived copies as well.

Public product documentation, technical specifications, reproducible test plans,
and operating instructions may remain in the repository. Keep them self-contained
and do not use them to reproduce private team strategy or schedules.
